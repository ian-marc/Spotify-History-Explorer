Listening over time

Listening time prioritizes recorded Spotify playback milliseconds. If that field is missing, a fallback estimate is attempted only when a track duration and a plausible next-timestamp interval are available. Combined mode normalizes each series separately so plays and hours can be compared without adding unlike units.
